A resubmission (v2) for "The 3AM Fridge Text": a one-line text message from a smart fridge to its owner at 3am. This entry drops the spent missing-cake angle of round 1 and flips the roles instead — the fridge stages a wellness intervention on the human raiding it: "3AM: this is an intervention. I'm a fridge and even I think you need to go back to bed." The laugh comes from a machine with no business having opinions deciding that 3am is the moment to speak, and from the sting of "even I": the appliance considers itself the sensible one in the room. Short enough for a lock screen, complete without a follow-up.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
